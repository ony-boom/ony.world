> Someone or something that is reliable can be trusted or believed because he, she, or it works or behaves well in the way you expect.
> 
> <cite>Cambridge Dictionary</cite>

It's been 4 months since I moved out of my parents' house. It's taught me a lot, but the biggest thing, the one that stuck, is that I now have a small, real sense of what being reliable actually means.

It's like that meme (not really a meme), you know the _"_[_I understand it now_](https://youtu.be/H3MgcNJG8J8?si=6T72gUbBbeH0glPf)_"_ one. Not quite there yet, but I'm starting to get it.

And so I've been thinking about how to actually apply it, not for anyone else, but for myself.Being someone I can count on. At work, in my hobbies, in the small everyday things. All of it.
